# Credits

## Audio
All music and sound effects in `Assets/Audio` are **original and procedurally generated** by
`tools/audio/generate_audio.py` (Python, numpy/scipy, OGG Vorbis via ffmpeg/libvorbis).
No samples, recordings, or third-party/CC0 assets are used. Vocal-like sounds (hit_ouch, ghost_wail,
werewolf_roar, boss_hurt) are synthesized from oscillators and filters and are intentionally non-human.

### LOOPS
These clips are authored as seamless loops. Set `AudioSource.loop = true` when playing them
(Unity's AudioImporter has no loop setting; looping is set per AudioSource):
- `Assets/Audio/Music/main_theme_loop.ogg`
- `Assets/Audio/Music/boss_theme_loop.ogg`
- `Assets/Audio/SFX/broom_wind_loop.ogg` (Free Flight wind bed, generated by `tools/audio/generate_wind.py`)

Every other clip (stings and all other SFX) is a one-shot (`loop = false`, or use `PlayOneShot`).

### Free Flight broom wind (suggested wiring)
- `broom_wind_loop.ogg`: play continuously on its own AudioSource with `loop = true`, started at volume 0.
  Each frame, with `speed01 = speed / maxSpeed` smoothed (for example `Mathf.SmoothDamp` or a lerp over ~0.15 s):
  `volume = Mathf.Lerp(0f, 0.8f, speed01)` and `pitch = Mathf.Lerp(0.8f, 1.3f, speed01)`.
  It is authored to work from pitch 0.8 to 1.4. At volume 0.8 it sits about 2 dB under the in-game main-theme level.
- `broom_turn_whoosh.ogg`: optional soft one-shot (`PlayOneShot`). Trigger it on sharp heading changes (for example
  more than ~60°/s of turn rate, or a direction reversal) with a cooldown of about 0.35 s, and scale it by speed01.
- The old `flap_whoosh.ogg` is unused in Free Flight; it is left in place.
